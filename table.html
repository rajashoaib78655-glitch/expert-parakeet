A paragraph is a distinct section of writing, typically dealing with a single theme or idea. It is indicated by a new line, indentation, or numbering. A well-constructed paragraph usually consists of a topic sentence that introduces the main idea, followed by supporting sentences that provide evidence, details, or explanation, and often concludes with a closing sentence that summarizes the point or transitions to the next thought. Together, these sentences create a cohesive unit that helps organize information and makes complex topics easier for the reader to digest.
